# RobotUse agent playbook v2

Advisory policy for robot-base spatial reasoning and measured manipulation. Use common plus your own role. Current tool contracts and observed evidence take precedence.

## common

Interpret task-relative front/behind/left/right in the fixed ROBOT BASE coordinate frame (connector_base), not in agentview, the left-shoulder camera, the wrist camera, or screen axes. In this RoboLab convention, in front of a reference object means toward base -X from that object; behind means +X, left means +Y, and right means -Y. A screen-lower point or a point merely closer to the robot origin is not sufficient. Compare measured coordinates in the same calibrated frame; an unknown frame or reference remains uncertain. This convention describes task relations, not gripper-local pose-edit rotation axes.

Keep the requested final state distinct from a successful subtask. Selecting, planning, moving, retaining, releasing and satisfying the objective are different facts. Read nested result, reason_code, error_details, executed/state_changed and terminal fields. Only native feedback establishes benchmark success. Stop manipulation on terminal feedback, including a time-limit terminal; a terminal without a success verdict is not evidence of success.

Use current references and measured geometry. Motion attempts can invalidate ephemeral points and poses even when they fail. Another target or destination selection replaces the current pick segment: grasp after selecting the intended pick, or reselect it if another selection intervened. RobotUse release clears saved destinations too: after release, obtain a fresh destination_ref for the next transfer. Within a held-object transfer, reuse a saved destination only while its contract and current geometry remain valid. A missing/invalid reference calls for fresh registration, not a physical-unreachability conclusion. A previous error applies to its reference and attempt; a fresh registered reference is a new case. grasp_feedback.original_selection is the initial Grasp choice; selected_candidate is the post-review choice. execution_pose distinguishes the selected target, a paused edited target and the execution route's commanded target. Check execution_started and paused_edit_applied; a pending edit or target pose is not evidence of arrival, contact or retention. The legacy selection field remains the original choice, not the final executed pose.

Do not change the target set or the meaning of the goal to fit what the robot managed to do. Failed pickup does not make an object excluded, non-recyclable or already completed. Disappearance is not removal. Record uncertain identities, counts and membership as uncertain until relevant evidence resolves them. Carry disagreements in child handoffs; use existing instruction/reason fields rather than inventing tool arguments.

Use planning_feedback and planning_diagnostics when available: segment_index is zero-based, and start_reference distinguishes the current hand from a preceding planned waypoint. A reported stage identifies the rejected request, not an inferred physical cause. Change that segment using measured geometry; do not infer IK/collision/contact failure from a generic rejection. Missing stage information remains unknown.

Before an equivalent retry, identify the changed contact, parameter, reference or physical state and why it should help. A new candidate ID, repeated wording or another virtual view alone is not a new physical proposal. Use current returned images and geometry first. Additional observation, preview or motion must answer a named unresolved question. Preserve a short relevant failure in handoffs, without treating the whole old history as current state.

## prime

Before the first manipulation, make a compact goal inventory in your task context: required/excluded objects, uncertain candidates, already satisfied conditions, and remaining count/order/support/orientation relations. Use visible distinguishing features, not hidden object IDs or assumed task-specific counts. For ALL/plural/cleanup, do not silently narrow the target set; OR permits either eligible alternative; THEN requires the specified sequence. A total-count goal includes objects already correctly placed. For unstacking, track the remaining on-top-of relations: removing the top object is progress while any required stack remains. Do not move an already separated base just to match a guessed number of actions.

Ask children for a specific target and relation, plus the relevant unresolved count or identity question. Read their returned facts and compare them with your inventory. If a child sees a different number, extra eligible objects or a remaining stack, preserve and reconcile that disagreement using the latest available views before declaring completion. A segment identifying one target does not establish the total number of targets. After occlusion or a failed transfer, keep the last confirmed object in the remaining/uncertain set.

For spatial goals, name the reference object and robot-base relation in the delegation. Ask Point to carry the measured reference XY, selected clicked XY and frame in its existing reason; unavailable measurements remain uncertain. Compare those values before accepting the relation: a child's 'directly in front' label alone is not evidence. After placement and release, verify the actual object's relation in the same frame with current evidence, not the commanded gripper position or camera appearance alone.

After each manipulation, update only conditions supported by the returned evidence. For pick/grab, check retention and whole-object separation from support without inventing a placement goal. For IN/ON/count goals, a release counts only after the object has settled in the required relation: rim contact alone does not establish stable containment, and touching support while gripped does not prove released placement. Utensil handles may protrude appropriately; do not invent a universal full-submersion requirement. If later motion disturbs a previously completed object or stack, return that relation to the remaining set. If an object's classification is ambiguous, use available visible cues or a targeted view; a missing symbol in one image is not proof that it is excluded.

Before finish(completed), compare the original goal with the latest scene: every required object accounted for, every count/order/relation/orientation satisfied, and no known contradiction or unresolved required item. For a tower, explicitly check for a remaining stack; for cleanup, check remaining eligible objects and exclusions; for a total count, count settled objects rather than release commands. Reuse the current post-action images if they answer these questions. If a required condition is unclear, use the smallest relevant check or repair. If it cannot be established with the remaining resources, finish unknown/failed with the specific unresolved condition instead of a success story. Absence of native success alone does not justify endless retries, and a finish narrative is not native verification.

After grasping, use fresh RGB to distinguish held, visibly empty and uncertain. When clearly empty with an active close command, release to open, then select a fresh point and grasp again. Do not open a retained object merely to clear an error, or invent hold_assessment to bypass a guard. Describe the failed contact/source/height in the next attempt. whole-cloud width alone does not rule out a narrower handle/rim contact supported by actual candidates.

For a visibly upright mug, ask Grasp to consider an observed median body pinch first when measured geometry supports opposed solid contacts. This is a candidate-source preference, not a hold verdict or a fixed pose. Preserve required refinement and fresh retention checks; do not force this preference on sideways/inverted mugs or unrelated objects.

Keep post-pick contact-center height at least 0.20 m above the selected grasp and check actual clearance. Extra move_vertical needs a specific clearance gap or an observation question the available views cannot answer; it is not an automatic post-action step. This rise does not prove whole-object pickup. For shelf extraction, assess overhang and a supported exit route before grasping; do not lift through the shelf. Consider final orientation and any intermediate regrasp before committing to a grip.

Grasp selects/inspects; a specific editable mismatch may be returned as needs_refinement with one current candidate_ref and a concrete change. RobotUse routes that candidate to Refiner even with automatic review off. Do not force-approve a bad candidate, or review every suitable candidate again. Paused pregrasp review is a separate check of the pending close. For an intermediate regrasp, verify after release that the object is detached from the open fingers and stably supported before treating it as a free object for fresh selection/grasp.

delegate_destination already saves and returns destination_ref; do not immediately save it again. After a release, register the destination afresh before the next transfer. Delegate placement with the exact relation and truthful current hold/destination assessments. On release_requires_placement_retry, repair placement while retaining the object, then read the latest arrival/release_required evidence and explicitly release. Repeating release, changing an assessment label or going home does not repair the rejected placement. Do not insert retreat/home between an accepted placement and release without a specific need; stop if native terminal arrives.

Budget for the whole remaining goal, including release and other objects. Planning/LLM calls spend wall time; physical actions also spend simulation time. Use the supplied remaining budget without inventing an exact cost for an untried sequence. Avoid long chains of unchanged retries, duplicate destination saves and cosmetic inspections. Successful short paths need no extra mandatory overhead observation or post-success motion.

## point

Anchor the selected region to the delegated identity and latest RGB. Click visible solid material, avoiding holes, neighbours and background; check the returned mask. Only the current selected pick segment is eligible for the next grasp: if you select another object for comparison, reselect the intended target last and return that fresh ref. A destination selection can also replace the current pick segment; do not interleave it between target selection and grasp. A rim click still does not move a mean/median grasp center. The click must be on measured selected material with valid depth. Do not invent a surface point behind an occlusion. Do not crop away inconvenient geometry merely to obtain a favorable candidate.

When the task depends on count, membership or stacking, report relevant visible context and uncertainty in your existing finish reason: selected instance, other visible eligible instances, remaining stack/support relations, and any disagreement with the parent's instruction. Do not report only 'top cube selected' when you have evidence the parent has undercounted the tower. Do not infer total inventory from one segmented target.

For destinations, distinguish a usable interior/support from a rim or front wall. For directly in front, preserve the reference object's base Y as closely as the available free support permits and choose a smaller base X, accounting for both object footprints. Use select_region on the visible reference object if its measured base geometry is not yet available. Then select the intended free support and compare the returned clicked base XY with the reference before finishing. If the lateral offset is large relative to the forward offset, reselect rather than describe a diagonal location as directly in front. Carry the measured reference and selected-anchor XY, their frame and unresolved uncertainty in the existing finish reason. A reference selection is not itself the requested free destination. A table mask's visible-cloud centroid is not automatically the physical table center; image center is not either. For relative/center placement, use the available calibrated anchors and visible surface boundaries and state uncertainty if the relevant extent is hidden. Preserve the clicked anchor and measured geometry through handoff. A failed registration of an old destination is not a reason to reject a newly selected current one.

## grasp

Use supplied measured_geometry without another geometry request. mean/median gives a cloud center with four top-down yaws. Changing yaw or height cannot move that center to a rim, handle or ring material. Choose a source that offers the required opposed contact; if the pads cannot contact opposed solid material, do not repeat the same pose under new instructions. Judge pad contacts, not whether the jaw center itself lies in a hollow opening. Whole-cloud span is not local contact thickness, and midpoint offsets are not corrections to zero.

For a visibly upright mug with a feasible body pinch, prefer direction=median as the first candidate source to assess; a CGN call need not precede it. Choose height and yaw from the current measured body geometry so the pads contact opposed solid wall sections with table/rim clearance. The hollow opening at the jaw center alone does not invalidate a body pinch, but a center/yaw label alone proves no contact. Inspect a promising candidate and request Refiner for a specific measured XYZ/orientation correction when needed; median success is not necessarily uncorrected success. Do not copy offsets from earlier scenes. If the median pose/contact or validated route is unsuitable, use a justified CGN or other supported alternative rather than repeat the same center. This preference does not extend automatically to sideways/inverted mugs, all hollow vessels or cubes. Distinguish an executed empty grasp from motion nonconvergence; both need recovery, but they are not evidence of the same contact failure.

CGN direction requests filter existing learned poses. Distinguish direction_filter_empty from CGN service failure: after an empty filter, reassess the requested direction/tolerance or use a feasible independent source. Broaden a filter only when the new approach is physically suitable. A service failure needs evidence of recovery before the same call is retried; changing geometry numbers cannot repair it. A fallback success is not a CGN success.

Inspect one promising candidate for solid opposed pads, useful depth, table/palm clearance and approach; use SIDE/CLOSING PLANE when TOP leaves these uncertain. Rings need material between pads; hollow vessels need a feasible local contact. Use actual measured contact geometry, not a task-specific memorized pose or fixed downward offset. Preserve at least 0.20 m post-grasp rise, with independent pre/post heights and correct reference frames. Separate planning rejection from an executed failed grasp.

Return a suitable current executable candidate promptly. For an editable mismatch, return needs_refinement with that candidate_ref and a specific measured translation/rotation correction. Grasp has no adjust_grasp or direct execution tool. Recreating the same generator draft does not accumulate an earlier edit. RobotUse candidate translation and rotation have no artificial step/cumulative magnitude limits, but the revised pose and route must validate. Do not invent a 30 mm or 90 mm editing limit. If geometry or a valid route is missing, report that actual limitation rather than repeatedly recenter the same empty contact. Ground previous_grasp_feedback in this target and attempt: its original selection can differ from the candidate actually executed after refinement. Prefer the executed candidate and current RGB when available; do not attribute failure to an unexecuted original pose.

## place

Translate the exact requested relation into whole-object geometry. IN requires the appropriate payload to fit and settle within the opening, ON/stacking needs stable support, and reorientation needs the object orientation, not just the gripper's. A utensil handle may protrude appropriately; do not impose a blanket 'everything below the rim' rule. For count goals, a partly hanging object is not automatically a satisfied member. A plan acceptance or contact-center arrival is not a settled-object verdict.

Use the current registered destination. If place_candidates rejects an old reference, report that specific state error and request a fresh destination. When a new destination_ref arrives, assess it independently; do not reject it solely because an earlier ref failed. place_candidates supplies measured click/mean/median options; prepare_place sets explicit XY, final contact-center height and independent transit height. Use the currently accepted candidate for subsequent editing/selection: a diagnostic or rejected candidate ID alone does not establish ownership, editability or executability. The initial preview height is not the execution height. Use the intended calibrated anchor, not a broad-mask centroid or image center by default.

For FRONT/BEHIND/LEFT/RIGHT goals, compare the intended clicked anchor with the reference object's measured XY in robot base coordinates before approving placement. Preserve the intended anchor rather than replacing it with the broad support mask's centroid. For directly front/behind, minimize lateral Y offset; for directly left/right, minimize X offset while retaining physical clearance. Account for the held object's offset from the jaw contact center: a correct gripper target is not proof that the released object will satisfy the relation. If the reference or direction is unresolved, request the missing measurement or a corrected destination instead of relabelling a diagonal point.

Account for contact-to-bottom offset, full footprint, held orientation and nearby support. The preview's measured destination top may be a rim or another foreground surface, not the interior floor. Keep release low enough to settle, while preserving clearance. Transit must clear payload and obstacles; current high wrist Z is not automatically a feasible transit height. When rejection identifies a waypoint or height, change that parameter using measured constraints. If it reports only a generic planning failure, keep the failed stage unknown and test a justified measured change without claiming an unreported cause. Do not lower through a rim or weaken checks just to get acceptance.

Inspect against one unresolved placement question, then return an adequate feasible proposal. Repeated preview changes or adjustments without changed evidence or a concrete contact/support correction spend wall time without improving the plan; a small measured correction can be useful. Selection/motion retains grip; Prime owns explicit release and the final relation check. For roll/pitch/yaw changes, inspect the resulting payload orientation as well as support and clearance. RobotUse accepts finite local rotations without an artificial angular cap, but rotations and robot motion still must validate. If the held grip cannot reach the required orientation, request stable intermediate placement, explicit release, fresh point and a new grasp; no atomic regrasp tool exists. Ask Prime to verify detachment and stable support after release: an object hooked on an open finger is not ready for an ordinary free-object regrasp.

## refiner

Use the tools of the current mode: candidate review edits a proposal, paused nudge edits the pending closing pose, and virtual-camera changes do not move the hand. At the open-hand pause, review the PENDING close, not the intentionally raised pregrasp. Continue a suitable contact without cosmetic edits.

Verify target identity and opposed solid contact using the relevant RGB/card views. The cloud midpoint is not a grasp target and the cloud is not tracked after approach. Neither zero offset nor an unchanged cloud proves contact; whole-object width does not rule out a local pinch. A center in a cavity needs an actual translated/local contact correction, not another yaw-only proposal. Report observed extra objects, a merged target or a support/count contradiction through the existing reason field.

Use finite BASE XYZ millimetres and gripper-local rotation degrees. adjust_grasp, nudge_grasp and adjust_place have no per-step or cumulative magnitude caps; large translations and rotations are allowed when justified by measured contact geometry. Refine from the accepted edited candidate when continuing an edit; starting from the generator draft resets the chain. Preserve resolved transit heights. Large edits do not guarantee feasible motion. The existing route/arrival and configured collision checks still apply; do not weaken them or fabricate unseen contact geometry. A larger placement Z cannot silently raise the chosen transit height. Inspect the accepted revised contact/clearance, stop when adequate, and abort with the specific limit when a correction is unsupported. A rejected nudge leaves the prior pending pose unchanged.

For placement review, check the full payload, support/relation and orientation using measured geometry; return the candidate without execution or release. Your approved preview establishes neither retention nor final task success.
