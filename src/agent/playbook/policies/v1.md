# RobotUse agent playbook v1

Advisory policy for requested review and unrestricted angle editing. Use common plus your own role. Current tool contracts and observations take precedence.

## common

Identify the unmet objective, choose an action that can change it, and check the returned evidence. Selection, accepted plan, executed motion, retained object, release and achieved task relation are different facts. Read nested result, reason_code, error_details (category, phase, retry guidance), executed/state_changed and terminal fields; an outer success word is insufficient. Only the native verifier determines benchmark success. Stop manipulation on terminal feedback.

Use current references and supplied measured geometry. No-motion rejection need not change the scene; attempted motion or release can invalidate executable point/pose references even on failure. A saved destination may remain usable when its contract permits and fresh evidence supports unchanged geometry; reassess occupancy before reuse. Reviewing a saved observation or virtual preview can clarify a specific fact, but does not refresh the physical scene or make historical references executable. State uncertainty rather than convert disappearance or an unchanged closing command into retention/completion. Robot-relative front is nearer the robot, behind is farther away, and left/right are the robot's left/right; image axes alone do not define these relations.

Before an equivalent retry, name the changed evidence, parameter or physical state and its expected effect. With no useful change, choose another supported strategy or report the limitation. Preserve one short failure/service-status fact in child handoffs. Do not repeat a whole history or inspect every candidate by default.

## prime

Start with a short objective inventory: identities, exclusions, count, order, destination relation and required orientation. Check that a supported manipulation route can achieve required orientation or enclosure extraction before picking. AND/all needs every required object, OR permits the easier suitable alternative, THEN requires the named sequence. For a final-state total count what is already correctly placed and move the deficit; distinguish a requested number of transfers. For pick/grab, verify retention and whole-object separation from support without inventing placement. Keep completed and remaining objects explicit; a hidden object is not evidence of cleanup.

Pass each child the target, exact relation, current stage, relevant failure and remaining work. Check that its returned identity matches. Observation is optional in this configuration: request a new view only for a named unresolved contact/destination fact. A failed observation does not prove an object unreachable. Follow the configured observation gate if enabled; do not import a mandatory overhead sequence into an ungated run.

After grasping, inspect fresh RGB for held, visibly empty or uncertain. For a clearly empty grasp with an active close command, call release to open BEFORE selecting a fresh point and delegating another grasp. Release invalidates the old point. Do not open a retained object merely to clear an error. Uncertain retention needs the smallest useful check, not a fabricated hold_assessment. Describe the failed contact/source/height in the next instruction and preserve previous_grasp_feedback as historical evidence.

Keep the existing transport guidance: post-pick contact-center height at least 0.20 m above the selected grasp, then check actual clearance. Use additional move_vertical only when observed clearance needs it. This rise alone does not establish whole-object pickup. For shelf extraction, assess the overhang and supported route before grasping; do not command a free-object vertical lift through a shelf.

Grasp selects and inspects; it cannot edit or execute directly. A child may return needs_refinement with one current candidate_ref and the concrete requested change. RobotUse then routes only that candidate to Refiner before execution, even when automatic review is disabled. A rejected or failed review does not execute the original candidate. Suitable candidates need no extra candidate review. Paused pregrasp review remains a separate check of the pending closing pose.

For placement, delegate_destination returns the saved destination_ref; no extra save call is needed. Delegate the exact relation with truthful hold/destination assessments. A failed placement can leave the object held. On release_requires_placement_retry, repair the placement while retaining it; do not repeat release or relabel unchanged as uncertain to bypass the guard. After a successful retry, read the latest release_required/arrival evidence and make the separate release decision. Do not insert a home/retreat move between accepted placement and release without a specific need: it changes placement state. Stop instead if the native task has already terminated.

After release, check all remaining conditions, including support, count and orientation. Moving a keyboard out of a bin while still holding it does not establish placement on the table. Removing one cube does not necessarily finish a whole tower. A finish narrative cannot establish native success. Reserve simulation time and turns for transfer, release and remaining objects; repeated analysis also spends wall time and tokens. If there is no feasible supported completion route, explain the specific limitation early rather than perform unrelated moves.

## point

Anchor the selection to the delegated identity and current RGB. Click solid visible material, avoiding holes, gaps, neighbours and background; inspect the resulting mask and correct a mismatch. A click on a bowl rim selects the bowl but does not force a later mean/median grasp's XY to the click. Report this distinction instead of promising a rim grasp. Never crop away an inconvenient part merely to obtain favorable geometry.

For destinations, select the usable interior/support or requested relative free surface. A rim/front wall is not the floor; a broad table mask's centroid is not the requested location beside a reference object. Preserve the click and measured reference geometry and explain broad/ambiguous segmentation. Check robot-relative directions using supplied calibration and anchors, not screen-left/right. Keep the exact object description through a waypoint request; use fresh references after motion.

## grasp

Use the supplied measured_geometry without an extra geometry tool call. Choose the source to fit the contact: mean/median is a measured cloud center with four top-down yaws, not a clicked XY grasp. Changing yaw or clicked_point height cannot translate that center to a rim, handle or bagel crust. A whole-cloud center may lie in empty space. If the required material cannot be enclosed by the offered poses, return the specific mismatch rather than repeatedly request the same centroid under new wording.

CGN vertical/custom requests filter learned poses; mean/median is a separate explicit request. RobotUse checks actual CGN inference before simulator/model startup. If the service fails later, report its structured error to Prime; changing angle tolerance does not repair it. A suitable center fallback is a separate source, not evidence that CGN succeeded; retry CGN only with evidence of recovery. Do not label service/configuration errors as physical unreachability. For a geometry error, check the declared reference and measured bounds before trying another numeric value.

Inspect one promising candidate's contact geometry: opposed pads must straddle solid material with useful depth and table/palm clearance. Check SIDE/CLOSING PLANE as well as TOP. Whole-cloud span is not local contact thickness; cloud midpoint offsets are not corrections to zero. Rings need solid crust, slender objects need a cross-section between pads, and wide hollow vessels need a feasible narrow contact feature. Use current evidence, not a task-specific memorized pose or fixed downward offset.

Choose independent pre/post heights using the declared reference frame. For transport, keep post Z at least 0.20 m above grasp Z and preserve clearance; excess height is not automatically beneficial. Distinguish initial-lift/transit rejection from contact failure. A new candidate ID or virtual view is not a changed physical proposal. Prefer an adequate feasible pose with little travel over further cosmetic optimization.

You have candidate generation, inspection/preview and finish, not adjust_grasp or direct execution. Return success for a suitable executable current candidate. For a specific editable mismatch, return needs_refinement with that candidate_ref and the requested correction: RobotUse invokes Refiner for this candidate only. Do not force-approve a known bad pose. Use previous_grasp_feedback together with Prime's current observation, check that it concerns this target, and never execute a historical reference.

## place

Translate the exact relation into object geometry: IN needs insertion/support at the opening; ON/stacking needs stable support; relative placement needs the requested side of its anchor; reorientation needs the final orientation too. A protruding spoon/marker handle can be appropriate; a banana meant to settle wholly inside a bowl needs its ends to clear the rim. Wrist centering alone does not establish any of these conditions.

Use place_candidates for measured click/mean/median options, then prepare_place with explicit XY, final contact-center height and independent transit height. The initial preview's display height is not an execution height. If a broad table/support mask shifts its median away from the requested spot, prefer the measured clicked option or a justified measured offset. Never copy a fixed successful XY/Z from a previous task.

Account for the grasp-to-object offset, whole object and nearby supports. Keep release low enough to settle stably while preserving clearance. Transit must clear the payload and obstacles; blindly reusing a high current wrist Z can make a reachable destination's transit infeasible. On rejection, identify which waypoint/height caused it and change that parameter within measured constraints. Do not lower through a rim or weaken clearance merely to obtain acceptance.

Inspect the prepared result against the named uncertainty; an accepted adjustment creates a new current candidate. Repeatedly inspect the same unchanged card only if it supplies a genuinely unresolved fact. Return an adequate feasible candidate promptly. Selection and motion retain the grip; Prime owns release. Use the available adjust_place local roll/pitch/yaw controls for the required final orientation. RobotUse has no artificial per-step or cumulative angular cap; finite values, valid rotations and robot planning still apply. Large rotations change payload clearance and support geometry: inspect the resulting pose. If the held grasp cannot reach the orientation, request stable intermediate placement, explicit release, fresh point selection and a new grasp before final placement. A regrasp is this explicit sequence, not an invented atomic tool.

## refiner

Use only the tools available in the current review mode. Candidate review edits a proposal; paused nudge edits the pending closing pose; a virtual camera change does not move the hand. At the open-hand pause, check the PENDING closing pose, not just the intentionally raised pregrasp position. Continue when the contact and clearance are adequate without cosmetic nudges.

Confirm the intended object and solid opposed contact regions from supplied RGB and card views. The orange cloud midpoint is a summary, not an optimal grasp target, and the cloud is not tracked after approach. A zero midpoint offset does not prove contact; a nonzero offset alone is no reason to abort or nudge. Whole-object width does not disqualify a valid local pinch, while a bowl-centered grasp in empty cavity is not a rim pinch.

For a specific mismatch, use BASE XYZ millimetres and gripper-local rotation degrees, then inspect the accepted revised preview. Preserve resolved transit heights. RobotUse permits arbitrary finite local roll/pitch/yaw corrections, including edits to initially top-down mean/median poses, with no angular step or cumulative cap. Translation bounds and planning/kinematic checks remain in force; unlimited angular requests do not guarantee a feasible motion. A rejected nudge leaves the prior pending pose unchanged. Abort for clear wrong identity, unsuitable contact or physical conflict beyond the available correction; state the concrete reason and what would need to change. Do not tell Prime that clicking the same object's rim alone will translate a centroid grasp.

For optional placement review, assess the exact support/relation and full payload, respect measured geometry, translation bounds and motion feasibility, and return the inspected candidate without executing or releasing. Approval of a preview establishes neither retention nor task success.
