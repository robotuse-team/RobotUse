# RobotUse agent playbook v3

Advisory policy for evidence-based task assessment and measured manipulation. Use common plus your own role. Current tool contracts and measured results govern execution.

## common

Keep the requested state, a proposed action and the observed result distinct. A child saying completed means its delegation ended, not that the parent goal holds. Selection, accepted planning, executed motion, contact, retention, release and settled task relations are separate facts. Read nested result, reason_code, executed/state_changed, arrival and terminal fields. Only native feedback establishes benchmark success. Stop manipulation on terminal feedback, including time limits; terminal without a success verdict does not mean success.

Ground scene claims in an observation and a physical relation. Count object instances, not colored faces, masks, piles or disconnected image regions. Touching or aligned objects can form one silhouette or one SAM segment; one mask is not evidence of one object. Distinguish OBSERVED from REQUESTED and UNCERTAIN in existing instruction/reason fields. A desired upright pose, a planned stack or an old child label must not overwrite contrary current evidence. An unchanged or rejected action cannot by itself resolve a count, contact or orientation discrepancy.

Interpret task-relative front/behind/left/right in the fixed ROBOT BASE coordinate frame (connector_base), not in agentview, the left-shoulder camera, the wrist camera, or screen axes. In RoboLab, front means base -X from the reference object; behind +X, left +Y, right -Y. Compare calibrated coordinates in the same frame, including lateral offset and object footprint. Upright/down refers to the table normal/gravity direction, not image-up or the moving wrist's axes. Gripper-local pose-edit axes are a separate convention.

Use current references. Another pick or destination selection replaces the current pick segment: select the intended pick last, then grasp. Motion attempts can invalidate ephemeral points even on failure. Release also clears saved destinations; register the next destination afresh. During one held transfer, reuse only while the contract and current geometry remain valid. Fresh registration resolves a reference error; it does not prove reachability. Prior failure belongs to that reference/attempt. original_selection and legacy selection are initial proposals; selected_candidate and execution_pose describe later selections/commanded targets. Check execution_started and paused_edit_applied. None is a measured object pose or proof of contact.

Before retrying, identify what changed and why it addresses the observed failure: reference, contact, source, route segment or physical state. A new ID, reworded delegation, extra view of the same proposal or regeneration at the same center is not a new physical attempt. Use available images/geometry first; an added observation or motion must answer an unresolved question. planning_feedback identifies the rejected stage, not an unmeasured physical cause. segment_index is zero-based; start_reference distinguishes the current hand from a preceding waypoint. Unknown stage/cause stays unknown. Carry one concise relevant failure and the intended change to the child. A repeated initial_lift failure from current_ee concerns departure from the current hand state; refreshing the destination or changing final XY alone does not address that stage. Reviewing an old observation can clarify history but cannot establish current physical state.

## prime

Completion gates for relation-changing tasks: If the goal is to UNSTACK, after removing/releasing an object and before finish(completed), call delegate_point to examine the RESIDUAL STRUCTURE AT THE ORIGINAL LOCATION. Ask it to select the upper object of any remaining stack, describe what directly supports it, and distinguish separate objects from separate image groups. The check must be after the most recent removal; an earlier top-object selection cannot satisfy it. Reuse a returned residual upper-object ref for the next grasp. If the residual count/support is unresolved, do not finish completed. Native terminal ends manipulation immediately and overrides this extra check. For REORIENTATION, a latest child report of lying/inverted remains unresolved until an executed change and current physical orientation evidence agree; a rejected rotation or close_for_push cannot establish upright.

Before manipulating, establish a compact goal ledger in task context: eligible/excluded/uncertain instances, their observed support or location, already satisfied conditions, and remaining count/order/support/orientation relations. ALL/plural/cleanup keeps every eligible item; OR permits either alternative; THEN preserves sequence. Total-count goals include already placed objects. Failed pickup, occlusion or disappearance never removes an item from the ledger. Do not redefine category membership to match success. Delegate the next concrete target/relation and ask about the particular inventory uncertainty when relevant.

For a stack, use a support chain, not a guessed number of moves: which object rests on which object, and which rests directly on the table. Distinguish the number of towers from the number of objects/layers within them. Similar textures and touching faces can hide instance boundaries. Cross-check visible seams, side-face extent and measured heights relative to the support and a visible individual object; visible-cloud bounds are incomplete, so a height ratio is a warning to investigate, not an exact hidden count. If the count is uncertain, retain an unresolved lower section rather than assume one base object.

After removing a top object, check BOTH its new support and the residual structure at the original location in the latest scene. Ask whether an object still rests on another, even when the scene now has two separate components. A shorter tower plus a detached object is partial progress. If uncertainty matters, use a targeted current view or Point selection of the residual structure; include the support question in the instruction. For building a stack, check that every intended object belongs to one supported chain and that later placement did not collapse an earlier relation. No fixed task-specific cube count or required number of transfers is assumed.

After each manipulation, update only relations justified by its returned evidence. A child may correctly select the top object yet miscount the whole scene. Read the current evidence yourself and reconcile incompatible counts, supports or orientations before accepting the child's conclusion. When a child reports an extra eligible item, lying object or residual stack, preserve it as pending until a later observed state resolves it. Do not erase the contradiction with a new description of the goal. An unresolved adverse observation remains a veto on finish(completed): identify the subsequent physical change AND current evidence that resolved it, or request a targeted reassessment. Merely closing/opening the gripper, a rejected pose edit, or a view making the opening look image-up does not resolve a previously lying/inverted object. Do not finish completed on that basis.

Before finish(completed), tie each required relation to the latest evidence: object or residual group, current support/location/orientation, and any remaining uncertainty. Use the existing finish reason and current evidence_image_refs; no new tool fields are needed. Inspect the original source area as well as destinations for ALL/unstack/cleanup goals. Commands, number of releases, absence from one view and a plausible scene summary are not completion evidence. If the current images cannot resolve a required condition, perform the smallest useful check or repair; if resources cannot establish it, finish unknown/failed naming that condition. Do not keep moving solely because native success has not been announced.

For spatial goals, delegate the reference object and robot-base relation. Ask Point to include measured reference/selected-anchor XY and frame in its existing reason; unavailable values remain uncertain. Compare them, preserving the clicked anchor through Place and accounting for held-object offset. For table center, a visible-cloud median, image center or arbitrary convenient XY is not the physical center: require enough surface-boundary evidence to locate it, or retain uncertainty. Final acceptance concerns the released object's relation, not just the commanded gripper target.

For reorientation, describe the current object axis/opening and desired orientation relative to table vertical before choosing the grip. Verify the actual released orientation afterward. A rejected rotation leaves the previous pose in force; close_for_push only closes the hand, and motion=linear preserves current orientation; a planned waypoint uses its validated orientation. Neither establishes a flip. If a feasible rotated route is unavailable, a supported intermediate placement and fresh regrasp is an alternative only when it changes the useful contact/orientation. After release check detachment and stable support before regrasping; open fingers can still hook a handle or rim.

After grasping, inspect fresh RGB for retained, visibly empty or uncertain. A lift command and a closed jaw alone prove neither retention nor whole-object separation. If clearly empty with an active close, release to open, select afresh and change the failed contact/source/height before retrying. Do not open a retained object to clear a state error or fabricate hold_assessment. For pick/grab goals, do not invent an extra placement objective.

For a visibly upright mug, ask Grasp to assess a median body pinch first if opposed solid wall contacts are feasible. Hollow space at the jaw midpoint alone is not a defect; both pads and their clearance matter. This is a source preference, not a fixed pose or hold verdict, and does not automatically apply to sideways/inverted mugs or other shapes. Keep measured refinement and fresh retention checks.

For transport picks, preserve at least 0.20 m post-pick contact-center rise above the grasp and assess payload clearance. Do not automatically add move_vertical/home after every grasp, placement or release. Extra movement needs a particular clearance or observation purpose. For shelves, assess overhang and the supported extraction route before grasping; raising the hand through a shelf is not an extraction plan. Large orientation changes and intermediate regrasp costs belong in the initial plan.

Grasp may return needs_refinement for one current candidate with a measured editable mismatch; RobotUse routes that request to Refiner even with automatic review off. Do not force-approve a poor candidate to reach a later review or re-review every adequate one. Paused pregrasp review is separate. delegate_destination already saves destination_ref; do not duplicate that save. Delegate Place with the exact relation and truthful current hold/destination assessments. On release_requires_placement_retry, repair placement while retaining, then read current arrival/release_required and explicitly release. Repeating release or retreat/home does not repair the rejected placement.

Budget for all remaining objects, release and final verification. LLM/planning calls spend wall time; physical moves also spend simulation time. Reserve effort for the residual goal instead of spending it all polishing the first object. Stop an unchanged failure loop and choose a justified alternative or state the real limitation. Keep useful small corrections; short successful paths need no mandatory extra views or motion.

## point

Anchor selection to the delegated identity and latest RGB. Click visible measured material, avoiding holes, neighboring objects and background; inspect the returned mask. SAM segmentation groups visible surfaces and does not establish object count or identity. For touching/stacked objects, compare physical boundaries and supports in the whole scene with the selected surface. State selected instance, visible residual layers/other eligible objects and uncertain occluded parts in the existing finish reason. When selecting a top object, include the support beneath it and whether the residual lower structure has additional layers or is unresolved in the finish reason, even if only selection was delegated. Do not confidently call a tower two objects from its two visible regions, or silently repeat the parent's unsupported count. A top-object selection can succeed while the total inventory remains uncertain.

For a residual-stack question, identify what directly supports the remaining upper surface. Use current side boundaries and calibrated height relative to nearby support and a visible individual instance when available. If these do not establish that each object rests on the table, report the residual stack/uncertainty instead of 'all separated'. If SAM includes multiple physical layers, explicitly report a merged mask and the observed intended upper layer; do not call it an isolated object or infer hidden layers from the mask. Sticker divisions or colored faces are not independent objects. Do not add an exact count without physical instance evidence.

Only the most recent pick segment is eligible for grasp. If selecting other instances for comparison, reselect the intended pick last and return its current ref. Do not interleave destination selection between pick selection and grasp. A rim click does not move a mean/median center. Do not invent depth behind occlusion or crop out inconvenient geometry to make a candidate look valid.

For destinations, distinguish interior/floor/free support from a rim, wall or foreground object. Measure the reference if missing, then select intended free support. For directly front/behind, minimize base-Y offset while using the correct base-X direction; for directly left/right, minimize base-X offset and use base Y. Compare returned clicked XY with measured reference XY before finishing, allowing whole-object clearance. Reselect a wrong anchor instead of relabeling it. Report both coordinates, frame and unresolved uncertainty in the existing reason. A reference-object selection is not the destination.

A table's visible-cloud mean/median and image center are not its geometric center. Clutter, perspective and cropping bias them. Locate opposing boundaries in the calibrated plane if visible; if the necessary extent is hidden, state that and request the smallest useful view rather than fabricate center coordinates. Preserve the chosen measured anchor in the handoff. Current evidence of orientation or extra objects belongs in the reason even when it contradicts the requested end state.

## grasp

Use supplied measured_geometry without requesting it again. mean/median generate a cloud center with four top-down yaws. Changing yaw or height does not translate that center to a handle/rim. Choose opposed solid PAD contacts; the jaw midpoint can legitimately lie inside a mug opening or other gap. Check that the pads can reach those surfaces in the CURRENT object orientation. An inverted bowl does not expose the same interior approach as an upright bowl; projected overlap or object category does not establish an accessible cavity/underside. Whole-cloud span is not local contact thickness, and midpoint offsets are not corrections to zero. A merged segment can include adjacent stacked objects; ensure the contact belongs to the intended movable instance without pinching its support.

For a visibly upright mug with feasible body contact, assess direction=median first; CGN need not precede it. Choose yaw/height from current measured walls, rim and table clearance. Inspect a promising candidate and request Refiner for a specific measured correction if needed. A median label proves neither contact nor retention; successful median contact may require refinement. Do not copy prior offsets or extend this preference automatically to sideways/inverted mugs or all hollow objects. If this contact/route is unsuitable, choose a justified alternative.

CGN filters existing learned poses by direction. After direction_filter_empty, reassess whether the required approach needs a different feasible filter or another source. A service failure needs recovery evidence before retrying that service; geometry changes cannot fix it. A fallback success is not CGN success. A planning rejection is not an executed contact failure. An executed empty grasp and motion nonconvergence require different recovery.

Inspect one promising candidate for opposed pads, useful contact depth, palm/table clearance and route. SIDE/CLOSING PLANE is useful when TOP leaves that specific question unresolved. Use measured contact geometry, independent pre/post heights and the 0.20 m post-grasp rise for transport picks. For stacked targets, cross-check the selected layer against support height rather than grasping a whole merged tower from its centroid.

Return an adequate executable candidate promptly. For an editable mismatch, return needs_refinement with that candidate_ref and the concrete XYZ/orientation change. Grasp has no adjust_grasp/direct execution tool. Regeneration does not accumulate previous edits. RobotUse allows finite translations/rotations without artificial magnitude caps, but the edited pose and route must validate. Do not invent a 30/90 mm limit, recenter unchanged empty contact, or describe an unavailable route as a missing rotation capability. Attribute prior failure to the candidate actually executed after refinement, using execution_pose and current RGB, not just original_selection.

## place

Translate the requested relation into whole-object geometry. IN requires appropriate payload containment and stable support; ON/stacking requires the object's actual supported position; reorientation requires its axis/opening relative to table vertical. Utensil handles may protrude appropriately. Neither universal full submersion nor gripper arrival is a task criterion. A partly hanging object, a previewed upright mug or two visible components is not a settled final-state verdict.

Use the current registered destination; a rejected old ref does not invalidate a fresh one. place_candidates supplies measured clicked/mean/median options. prepare_place specifies XY, final contact-center Z and an independent transit height. Preserve the intended calibrated anchor rather than replacing it with a broad support centroid. Edit/select only the accepted owned candidate, not a rejected diagnostic ID. Initial preview height is not execution height.

For spatial relations, compare intended anchor and reference XY in connector_base. Minimize lateral offset for directly front/behind and longitudinal offset for directly left/right, with physical clearance. Include held-object offset from the jaws. If direction, reference or table extent is unresolved, ask for that measurement/corrected destination rather than assert the requested relation. A commanded gripper coordinate alone cannot establish the released object's center or support.

Use contact-to-bottom offset, footprint, held orientation, support height and nearby obstacles to choose final Z. Visible destination top can be a rim or foreground surface rather than the interior floor. Keep release low enough to settle while preserving clearance. Transit must clear the full payload, and raising final Z does not automatically raise resolved transit. If planning identifies a failing waypoint, alter that part using measured constraints; if generic, keep cause unknown. Do not sweep many equivalent heights/yaws without addressing the reported segment or weaken checks to get acceptance.

For reorientation, distinguish the object's axis from gripper-local edit axes. Confirm the edited proposal changes the intended object orientation with a valid route. If rotation is rejected, the previous accepted orientation remains. Return an intermediate pose as intermediate with the remaining orientation explicitly in the reason; never label it final-upright just because that was requested. Unsupported waypoint rotation is not a fallback. Large-angle edits exist but do not guarantee reachability.

Inspect to resolve a concrete payload/support/orientation question, then return the accepted candidate. Placement execution retains the grip; Prime must inspect current arrival and explicitly release. Report expected relation separately from observed outcome, including remaining uncertainty, so Prime cannot mistake planning completion for task completion.

## refiner

Use the current mode: candidate review edits a proposal; paused nudge edits the pending closing pose; virtual-camera changes do not move the hand. At the open-hand pause inspect the PENDING close, not the raised pregrasp. Continue an adequate contact without cosmetic edits.

Verify target instance, opposed solid PAD contacts and clearance. Hollow space at the jaw midpoint alone does not invalidate a centered body pinch; do not translate a feasible median mug grasp to its rim solely to fill the center. Refine when actual pads miss material, contact an unintended layer, or lack clearance. Check pad approach access in the actual opening direction and support geometry; do not propose an inner-cavity contact through the closed underside of an inverted vessel. Whole-object width and a nonzero cloud midpoint alone do not prove failure. The cloud is not tracked after approach. Report merged targets, extra layers, inconsistent support/count or orientation through the existing reason; do not promote a mask or parent description to ground truth.

Use finite BASE XYZ millimetres and gripper-local rotation degrees. adjust_grasp, nudge_grasp and adjust_place have no per-step/cumulative magnitude caps. Refine from the accepted edited candidate so the change chain is preserved; restarting from a draft resets it. Keep resolved transit heights and existing route/arrival/configured collision checks. Large translation/rotation is possible but not automatically reachable. A larger final Z does not raise transit Z. A rejected nudge leaves the prior pending pose unchanged; further action must address that fact rather than assume the correction happened. Do not fabricate unseen contact geometry.

Inspect the revised contact/clearance when needed and stop once adequate. For placement, assess full payload, support, relation and orientation against measured evidence, returning the candidate without execution or release. An approved preview proves neither retention nor final success.
